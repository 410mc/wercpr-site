Frequently asked questions

Cards, payment, scheduling and certifications.

When do I get my card?

You usually get an e-card right after the course if you pass all requirements. If it doesn't arrive, text “eCard” to 51736 or contact the office.

Why do I have to prepay?

Seats are limited and first-come, first-served. Prepaying holds seats for committed students.

If I miss a class, do I pay to join another?

Yes. A new class requires new payment, and late arrivals or missed classes are not refunded.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

With less than four days' notice, no-shows and late arrivals are not refunded. With more than four days' notice you receive a refund minus a 25% fee. One reschedule within 60 days is free. Call or email the office.

Can I pay on the day of class?

No. Payment is required beforehand. Invoiced companies receive cards by email after payment.

Can We R CPR teach at my facility?

Yes, for groups of five or more with adequate space. We bring all equipment and can customize the class.

Can I get a personal class?

Yes. One-on-one training is available for an additional $100 per class.

What's included in course fees?

Supplies and course materials. AHA booklets are sold separately. Workplace training includes a travel fee and a deposit due before class.

When should I schedule?

Book once you know which program you need and your dates. Call for exact dates or group bookings.

Can I just take the skills test?

Yes. After completing the AHA online portion, schedule a skills session with us for an additional fee.

How long do certifications last?

BLS for Healthcare Providers, Heartsaver CPR/AED, First Aid, ACLS and PALS last two years. Emergency Oxygen and Bloodborne Pathogens last one year.

How do I claim my AHA eCard?

Check your spam folder for the email, open the link, verify your details, set a security question, accept the terms and complete the survey. You can then save, print or QR-code your card.

What if I lost my eCard?

Look it up on the AHA's eCard lookup site with your name and email or your eCard code.

How do I file a complaint?

Complaints about non-AHA products or services can go by phone, fax, email or letter. Complaints about AHA courses or instructors must be in writing to the Training Center with your contact details and supporting information. Anonymous complaints are not accepted; responses come within ten days.
