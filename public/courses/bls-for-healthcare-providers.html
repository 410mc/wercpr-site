BLS For Healthcare Providers

BLS For Healthcare Providers

Sign UpRenewal

The American Heart Association (AHA) BLS for Healthcare Providers Course is designed to teach healthcare professionals the skills of one- and two-rescuer CPR for victims of all ages, the use of an automated external defibrillator (AED), and the relief of choking. Upon successful completion of the post-test and skills demonstration, students will receive their AHA Healthcare Provider course completion card which is valid for two years. Course length 4 hours.

Intended Audience:

  • Physicians/Chiropractor/Podiatrist
  • Nurses-RN or LPN
  • Paramedics or Emergency Medical Technicians
  • Respiratory therapists
  • Nurse Practitioner
  • Occupational /Speech Therapist
  • Behavioral Therapist
  • Massage Therapist
  • Physical therapists
  • Physicians assistants
  • Medical or nursing students in training, aides, medical or CNA’s
  • Dental Workers (Dental Hygienist, Dental Assistant)
  • Pharmacist-Pharmacy Technologist
  • Students going into Nursing, Resp, PTA, OTA, Radiology, and such
